## Direction

Make collecting team signatures a fast, reliable iPad workflow: roster identity first, pen-only capture second, server-confirmed artifact state third.

## Core Rules

1. A collection is uniquely identified by canonical collection code and season. Men’s Basketball uses `MBB`, Football uses `FB`, and Volleyball uses `VB`; Creative staff use `CREATIVE`; one-off manual signers use `ADHOC` with their sport/category stored on the member.
2. External roster members are separate from internal Gear Tracker users. Creative staff are linked internal full-time users whose primary/assigned area is Video, Photo, or Graphics, or whose job title explicitly identifies Creative or Digital Media; they are represented by separate signature-member records in the standalone Creative staff collection. A uniquely matched same-season team-staff record shares that internal identity and resolves the same canonical Creative Staff capture, so saving or removing it from either roster updates both surfaces without copying private artifacts.
3. Only staff/admin users can access the area. Students and collaborators are denied by the centralized permission map.
4. Completeness is derived from active required members with a committed current artifact revision. Players always require a signature and cannot be excluded from readiness. Inactive and excluded support staff do not count; Creative staff in the standalone roster are included by default and can be excluded individually by an admin.
5. Roster imports are snapshots, not destructive syncs. MBB, Football, and Volleyball each use an allowlisted UWBadgers source adapter; Football and Volleyball map the `2026-27` season to the source site's `2026` roster URL segment. Apply is explicit, versioned, and never deletes historical members or captures. Creative Staff reconciliation starts automatically when `/signatures` mounts through the existing mutation endpoint; collection-list GET stays read-only so route prefetch cannot change roster state. Reconciliation remains version-checked and audits only real changes. It links team staff only on a unique exact normalized-name match among active eligible internal users and fails closed for players, ad-hoc members, ambiguity, or an existing conflicting link; team collections never contain Creative Staff members.
6. Capture input is normalized strokes. The server owns SVG/PNG generation, crop bounds, hashes, clean signer filenames, and private storage paths. PNG exports are transparent RGBA, preserve aspect ratio, render up to 1600 x 900, and are never less than 1000px wide. SVG exports contain sanitized vector paths rather than embedded raster data.
7. A pending or failed save is never presented as complete or downloadable.
8. Durable capture and artifact state remains in signature tables; the general audit log is supplemental evidence and is subject to its 90-day retention policy.
9. The active collection chooser hides archived seasons by default. Admins may reveal, archive, and restore historical collections; archived collections remain read-only.
10. Roster presentation keeps players in jersey-number order, renders actual player numerals in the licensed Wisconsin Athletics `WIsconsin-Regular` face, keeps coaching/support staff in the order preserved by the UWBadgers source snapshot, and sorts the standalone Creative staff roster by last name. Staff and Creative rows retain the person icon instead of a synthetic number.
11. Signature rosters use fixed-height horizontal rows with Person, Signature, and compact Actions rails. Person names use Gotham Black. A committed capture renders as a compact private PNG button that opens an authenticated Quick Look titled with the signer name and equal-height PNG/SVG downloads; an incomplete member renders the centered capture action in the same slot. Creative Staff rows show the person name only; imported players show normalized position and academic year (for example, `Guard • Senior`) beneath the name, while other team members retain their one-line source title. Player groups are labeled Student-Athletes, team staff group labels use title case, and multi-digit jersey numerals use restrained positive tracking. Roster groups are independently collapsible and carry their own signed count, so the detail-level readiness card and per-row Requirement/Status labels are omitted. Required capture actions use the official `#c80000` RGB-red brand treatment, while excluded non-player capture actions remain equally sized and available with neutral styling. Signature previews use display-only theme filtering so transparent source PNG bytes render black in light mode and white in dark mode. Secondary file, non-player readiness, and lifecycle actions remain available from the 44px compact row action menu. Explicit PNG and SVG actions download attachments with clean `<signer>-signature` filenames; all previews and downloads remain authenticated.
12. Admin capture settings are collapsed by default and explain how ink color, line thickness, trim margin, and maximum dimensions affect generated PNG/SVG output. Saved signatures lock the fields, while collection reset remains available in a separated danger area behind a descriptive confirmation.
13. Successful recaptures keep prior immutable private revisions available from the row menu with clean versioned filenames. Remove signature and collection reset erase every retained revision in their scope.
14. Staff/admin can add an ad-hoc signer from `/signatures` by entering a name, sport/category, and season; creation is audited and opens the capture surface immediately.
15. Signature capture is iPad-only. Unsupported web clients show a disabled `Capture on iPad` action with a tooltip explaining that an iPad and Apple Pencil are required; direct desktop visits to the capture surface are blocked.

## Runtime Surfaces

- `/signatures`: separate MBB, Football, Volleyball, Creative staff, and ad-hoc collection cards, completeness indicators, archive controls, a sport-aware UWBadgers import entry point, and one-off signer creation.
- `/signatures/[collectionId]`: roster grid, settings, reconciliation, and file library.
- `/signatures/[collectionId]/capture/[memberId]`: full-height pen-only capture with signer identity at the top.
- `/api/signatures/*`: authenticated collection, import, capture, artifact, and cleanup contracts.
